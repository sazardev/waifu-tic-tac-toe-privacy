Privacy Policy

My Waifus Tic Tac Toe Girls
Last updated: October 3, 2026

This game is developed by SazarCode ("we", "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The game works completely offline and does not require an account or internet connection.

Data Stored on Your Device

Game progress (levels, coins, unlocked galleries and settings) is stored only on your device using local storage. It never leaves your device, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

  • Vibrate – used for haptic feedback during gameplay.
  • Photos / Storage (save images) – used only when you choose to save a wallpaper or gallery image to your device.

The app does not request internet access, camera, microphone, location, or contacts.

Third Parties

We do not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or any third-party service that collects data. The game is distributed through Google Play; Google's own terms and privacy policy apply to the store transaction.

Children's Privacy

The game does not collect any data from anyone, including children. It is a general-audience game with anime-style characters.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above.
